School subjects descriptions

Mathematics
Mathematics is the study of numbers, shapes, and patterns. It helps develop problem-solving skills and logical thinking.
Science
Science explores the natural world through observation and experimentation. It includes subjects like physics, chemistry, and biology.
English
English focuses on reading, writing, and communication skills. It helps students express themselves effectively.
History
History is the study of past events and societies. It helps us understand how the world has evolved over time.
Physical Education
Physical Education promotes physical fitness and overall well-being through sports and exercise activities.
